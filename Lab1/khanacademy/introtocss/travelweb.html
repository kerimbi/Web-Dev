TRAVEL to TARAZ

Taraz is one of the oldest cities in Kazakhstan, located in the south of the country. It has a rich history that goes back more than 2,000 years, and it was an important stop on the Silk Road, where traders traveled between Asia and Europe.

    Places to Visit in Taraz
  • Aisha Bibi Mausoleum - a beautiful and historic landmark
  • Karakhan Mausoleum - an important cultural monument
  • Taraz Central Park - great for walking and relaxing
  • Taraz History Museum - learn about the city’s past
  • Talas River Area - peaceful place to enjoy nature
  • City Square - popular spot for events and photos
  • Local bazaars - experience local food and culture
  • Cafes and restaurants - cozy places to spend time with friends
